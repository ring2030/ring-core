# Welcome to the ring Academy

**Ages 11–18** (and mentors): in this course you will **rebuild** a tiny slice of the **ring Open Protocol (ROP)** — gaze or dwell selection, a nurse notification, and (later) a gentle AI line.

## What you will make

By the end you will have:

- A web page with **two call reasons** (like “bathroom” and “chat”).  
- A **dwell** or button path that feels fair for shaky hands or tired eyes.  
- A **Firebase** (or other) ping that shows up on a simple “staff board” page.

## Who this is for

- You speak any language at home — we start in **English** and **日本語** side notes.  
- You have a **mentor** (teacher, parent, Technovation coach) in the room for accounts that cost money or need phone numbers.

## Safety (read with your mentor)

ROP is **not** a medical device. Do **not** promise patients that your homework project will call a real ambulance. Ship demos to **friends and family** first.
