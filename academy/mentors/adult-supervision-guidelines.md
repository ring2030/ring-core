# Adult supervision guidelines (mentors)

## Before the first call with youth

- Confirm **parent/guardian** knows the schedule and recording policy.  
- Use **official meeting links** from the school or Technovation — no personal Zoom rooms if policy forbids it.  
- **Two adults** present when feasible (host + observer).

## During sessions

- **Camera on** for mentors; youth may keep camera off for privacy but then **voice only** in group setting.  
- Pause if anyone seems tired — this work is emotional.

## After sessions

- Write a **one-paragraph public recap** in GitHub Discussions (what was decided) so no “hidden” state exists.

## Red lines

- No **romantic** or **appearance-based** compliments toward minors.  
- No **one-on-one** social media follows from mentor personal accounts.

Thank you for making the ring Academy safe.
