# Cultural Notes — Japan (`ja-JP`)

## Language considerations

- Prefer **敬語 (keigo)** or gentle **です／ます体** for system prompts; avoid blunt imperatives toward distressed users.  
- Short **acknowledgement phrases** (“かしこまりました”, “少し待っていてね”) build trust better than long clinical disclaimers on every tap.

## Family structure

- Adult children often coordinate care; **do not assume** a spouse is the primary contact.  
- Offer **neutral relationship pickers** (子ども / 兄弟 / その他).

## Religious & spiritual context

- **仏式 / 神道 / キリスト教** diversity exists; end-of-life copy should stay **non-sectarian** and defer to staff.

## Healthcare system context

- Nurse-station workflows are highly ritualised; **sound + colour** cues should align with existing alarm UX guidelines where possible.

## Taboos & sensitivities

- **Pure white** gift-wrap associations vs UI: white-on-black minimal UI is generally acceptable for digital HUDs but test with local nurses.  
- Avoid **finger-pointing icons**; prefer abstract targets.
