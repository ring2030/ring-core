# Cultural Notes — United States (`en-US`)

## Language considerations

- Default to **simple active voice** (“Tap here”) but offer **gentler** variants for hospice (“Whenever you’re ready…”).  
- Avoid idioms that confuse **ELL** patients (“hang tight”, “no worries”).

## Family structure

- Document **HIPAA** nuances for family portals separately; ROP recipes only cover **UX tone**.

## Religious & spiritual context

- **Christian**, **Jewish**, **Muslim**, **Hindu**, **Buddhist**, **secular** patients coexist; never assume a chaplain’s availability from UI copy.

## Healthcare system context

- **Alarm fatigue** is endemic; pair gaze calls with **rate limits** and **aggregate dashboards** (PHIL).

## Taboos & sensitivities

- Red as **error-only** in some ICUs; validate with local nurse educators.
