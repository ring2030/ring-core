# Contributor Covenant + Child-Safety Addendum

## Our pledge

We are building assistive communication software as a **community of minors and adults**. We pledge to make participation a **harassment-free, grooming-free, and age-appropriate** experience for everyone.

## Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.  
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.  
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.  
- Focusing on what is best for the community and end users.  
- Showing empathy towards other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention **of any kind**.  
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.  
- Public or private **intimidation**.  
- Publishing others’ private information (doxxing) without explicit permission.  
- Conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a **youth-centred** setting.

## Child-safety addendum (mandatory for this project)

This addendum applies to **all** spaces where ROP / ring Open Protocol work happens (GitHub, chat, video calls, social media).

1. **No direct messaging between adults and minors.**  
   Technical discussion happens in **public** GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, or GitHub Discussions only. If a minor needs help, an adult mentor responds **in public** so that other mentors can observe.

2. **No off-platform coordination** between adult contributors and minors for project work (no WhatsApp/Telegram/Discord DMs for ROP tasks). Parents/guardians may use private channels with their own children—that is outside our scope.

3. **Parental / guardian visibility.**  
   Contributors under the age of majority SHOULD involve a parent or guardian when joining video calls or when sharing screen recordings.

4. **Grooming zero tolerance.**  
   Any attempt to isolate a minor, solicit private contact, or discuss unrelated personal topics will result in **immediate removal** and reporting to the platform provider and, where required by law, to authorities.

5. **Mentor duty.**  
   Adult “core mentors” MUST background-check per their organisation’s policy before being listed as official contacts.

## Reporting

Reports may be sent to the **maintainer security contact** (see [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md)). For urgent child-safety concerns, also use the **GitHub Trust & Safety** reporting tools.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct. Project leadership will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.
